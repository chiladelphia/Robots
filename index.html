    Open current handouts

    Register with FIRST

    Create or update the student profile, then request to join FRC Team 484.

    Show up and connect

More than a machine

Find your part of the team

A complete robotics team needs people who think in code, metal, stories, schedules, design, and community.

MECH

Design + build

CAD, prototyping, machining, wiring, assembly, and field repair.

CODE

Program + test

Robot controls, sensors, autonomous routines, data, and iterative testing.

OPS

Compete + organize

Drive team, scouting, strategy, logistics, safety, and pit operations.

STORY

Communicate + connect

Media, design, outreach, sponsorship, fundraising, and event production.
